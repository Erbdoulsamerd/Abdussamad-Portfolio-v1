Gidan Kasa

A place you can only really describe by sending someone a picture of it — so here are six, and what they are for.

Six pictures

Wish you were here. Genuinely.

Gidan Kasa was designed for the hour before sunset, when low sun rakes across clay and the walls stop being surfaces and start being light sources. Everything else — the plan, the palette, the furniture height — follows from wanting that hour to last.

It is a small building. Two bedrooms, a kitchen, a living room and a pool in the middle. The ambition was never scale; it was that you would want to write to someone about it.

Designed for the hour before sunset, and for the letter you write afterwards.
