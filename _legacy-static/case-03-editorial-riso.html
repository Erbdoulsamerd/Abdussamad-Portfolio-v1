Enlira · A letter on impact
On this project,

Enlira — An identity for a firm that puts institutional discipline behind social outcomes — written as plainly as it deserves.

Kind regards, Flint

The work

Impact deserves the same discipline as any other investment.

Impact investing has a presentation problem. It is usually dressed either as charity — soft, apologetic, watercoloured — or as finance, in which case the impact disappears entirely. Enlira needed to look like neither. The answer was a forest green as serious as any institution, a gold that carries the optimism, and a single continuous line drawing of a butterfly: transformation stated once, without a metaphor being laboured.

“Great impact is never built alone.”

Rules we set

  • 01Green is the institution. Gold is the outcome.
  • 02The butterfly is drawn in one unbroken line — never filled.
  • 03Set everything in Gilroy. Nothing decorative.
  • 04Show real people at work, not stock optimism.
