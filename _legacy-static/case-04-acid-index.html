THE SECRET INGREDIENT IS FRESHNESS.

Aadil's headlines are not set inside a layout — they are the layout. Gilroy at its heaviest, cropped tight to the frame, red on yolk yellow, with the bird pushing in from the edge. There is no decoration doing any work. The vocabulary is deliberately Nigerian and deliberately spoken. KUKURUKU is not a slogan, it is the sound a rooster makes. That single decision does more brand-building than a paragraph of positioning ever would.

Rules

01

Set it heavy, set it big, crop it tight.

02

Red type on yellow. Never the reverse.

03

Use the words people already say out loud.

04

Let the bird break the frame.
