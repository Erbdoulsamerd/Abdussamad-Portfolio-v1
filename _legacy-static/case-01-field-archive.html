Gidan Kasa

Hausa for “house of earth.” A hospitality concept where the ground the building stands on became the material, the palette and the name.

ProjectGidan Kasa
DisciplineHospitality concept
ScopeIdentity · Spatial · Interiors
StageConcept & visualisation
PlaceNorthern Nigeria
01Intent

Not a building with a logo attached.

Most hospitality projects are assembled in sequence: an architect draws the shell, an interior designer dresses it, and a brand studio arrives at the end to name it. Each step inherits decisions it had no part in making.

Gidan Kasa was approached the other way round. The name, the plan, the plaster and the language were resolved together — so you cannot separate the branding from the architecture, because there was never a moment when they were separate.

A space can influence behaviour. So it should be designed with the same intent as an identity.

01Build from what is already underfoot — earth, not import.
02Let the courtyard, not the corridor, organise the plan.
03Design for the hour before sunset, when the walls do the work.
04Make every surface something a hand would want to touch.
