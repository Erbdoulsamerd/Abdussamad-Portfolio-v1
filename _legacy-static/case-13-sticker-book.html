Freshness

A brand you'd want to peel off and keep.

The test for a consumer food brand is simple: would somebody stick it on a laptop? Aadil was built to pass that test. Rounded script, a watercolour rooster, a red comb, and a yellow warm enough to look edible.

Every asset was designed to survive being cropped, stickered, screenshot and reposted — because that is what actually happens to a food brand, whatever the guidelines say.

Lowkey the best decision today.
  • 01Round everything. No sharp corners except the comb.
  • 02Every asset must work as a crop.
  • 03Illustration is watercolour — the only soft thing in the system.
  • 04If it can't be a sticker, it isn't finished.
