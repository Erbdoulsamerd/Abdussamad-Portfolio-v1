A mark you could draw with a finger.

The Aadil logotype is written, not typeset — a single soft script, rounded at every terminal, with no sharp edge anywhere in it. The comb above the first letter is the only geometry that is allowed to be pointed, and it earns that by being the one thing that says chicken.

It was tested the way a mark should be tested: photocopied, faxed, shrunk to thumb size, embroidered onto a cap. It survives all of it, because there is nothing in it fine enough to lose.

“If it survives a photocopier, it will survive a delivery van.”
