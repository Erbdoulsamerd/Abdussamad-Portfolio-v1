Note 01 — backgroundSheet 03

I began in brand identity. I did not stay there.

Although I began my journey through brand identity design, my work has continually expanded beyond traditional boundaries. Today, I work across branding, creative direction, industrial design, photography, editorial design, spatial thinking, hospitality concepts, and community building. I don't see these as separate disciplines, but as different ways of expressing the same idea.

Design is not defined by the medium. The medium simply becomes the most appropriate tool for solving a problem.

Note 03 — field recordSheet 05

I have designed brand identities for businesses, startups, and cultural organizations. I have built physical structures like a skateboard ramp to support a growing skateboarding community. I have developed hospitality concepts where branding, architecture, interiors, and storytelling become one experience. Through photography, I document people, places, and ideas with the same intentionality that I bring to design.

Growing up in Northern Nigeria has significantly influenced my perspective. It has inspired me to explore local stories, architecture, youth culture, craftsmanship, and community through a contemporary design lens.

Note 04 — forward intentSheet 07

My ambition is to continue building projects that blur the boundaries between disciplines. I want to create brands that become communities, spaces that become destinations, products that become rituals, and ideas that leave a lasting cultural impact.

I don't believe great design lives in one category. I believe it lives in the connections between them.
