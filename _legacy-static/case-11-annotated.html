The decisions, in the order they were actually made — with the reasoning left in the margin rather than tidied away.

Modelled before it was drawn.

Working in the model rather than in elevation meant light could be tested directly. The sun was moved until the stair threw the right shadow, and the plan was allowed to settle around what had been found.

Most case studies present the result as if it were inevitable. It wasn't. The courtyard moved twice, the stair was straight for a fortnight, and the palette lost two colours before it worked.
