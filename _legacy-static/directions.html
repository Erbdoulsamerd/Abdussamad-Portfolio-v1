Fourteen directions

Every image in the reference folder was pulled apart and rebuilt into fourteen complete art directions for this portfolio. Each one is real HTML and CSS — not a flat mockup — so you can see exactly how the type, colour and grid behave. Each also has a full page you can scroll.

Click any preview to open it full-screen and step between them with the arrow keys. The site you are currently reading is built in Direction 01 — Field Archive. Pick a different one and the same three pages get rebuilt in it.

—How to choose

Pick the one you'd defend.

These are not ten skins on one layout — the grid, the type and the reading order genuinely differ. That means the choice is strategic, not decorative. A few honest notes on what each one costs you:

Loudest 04 Acid Index, 08 Signal Orange and 12 Reparations Band get remembered, but they compete with your work for attention.
Quietest 07 Studio Index and 01 Field Archive get out of the way — best if the imagery carries everything.
Warmest 13 Sticker Book and 14 Postcard read as generous and human — they lower the barrier to approaching you.
Cleverest 11 Annotated is the one an art director will remember, because the handwriting says something the layout can't.
Riskiest 02 Dossier, 06 Collage and 10 Photocopy are delightful once, and need real discipline to stay usable at scale.
Best fit 05 Blueprint if the spatial and industrial work becomes the centre of the practice.
Chosen one?

Let's build it out.
