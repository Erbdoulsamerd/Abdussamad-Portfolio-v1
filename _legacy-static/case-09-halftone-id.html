Identification series — the rooms, one card each.

The curved stair, the deep window reveals, the perforated parapet: all of it is calibrated for late afternoon. Furniture was kept low and long so nothing interrupts the wash of light across the render.

Bathrooms were treated as the test case. If a room with a drain in the floor can still feel like somewhere you would linger, the material logic is working.
