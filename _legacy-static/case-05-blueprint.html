General notesSheet 07

The courtyard does the organising.

The corridor is deliberately exterior. You feel the temperature change on the way to bed. It is a small inconvenience that does more for the experience of the place than any amenity could.

  • 01Circulation stays outside — 100% of it.
  • 02Every room opens onto the courtyard, not a hall.
  • 03Openings sized for late-afternoon sun, not noon.
  • 04Five materials, no more.

The whole scheme was resolved in three dimensions before it was drawn — moving the sun until the stair threw the right shadow, then letting the plan settle around what had been found. The drawing came last, as a record of a decision already made.
