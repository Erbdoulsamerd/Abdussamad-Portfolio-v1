02Statement

The strongest ideas are not confined to one discipline.

  • A A visual identity can shape perception.
  • B A product can encourage interaction.
  • C A space can influence behavior.
  • D A photograph can preserve culture.
  • E A community can change lives.

Although I began through brand identity design, my work has continually expanded beyond traditional boundaries. I don't see these as separate disciplines, but as different ways of expressing the same idea. Design is not defined by the medium — the medium simply becomes the most appropriate tool for solving a problem.
