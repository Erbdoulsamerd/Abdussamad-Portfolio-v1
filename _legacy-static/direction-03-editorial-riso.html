Dear reader,

I am a designer in Northern Nigeria and I do not work in one discipline, because the idea never stays in one. A visual identity shapes perception. A product encourages interaction. A space influences behaviour. A photograph preserves culture. I design systems people can walk into, not deliverables they receive.

Kind regards, Flint

On method,

Although I began through brand identity design, my work has continually expanded beyond traditional boundaries. I don't see these as separate disciplines, but as different ways of expressing the same idea. Design is not defined by the medium — the medium simply becomes the most appropriate tool for solving a problem.

— A. I.
